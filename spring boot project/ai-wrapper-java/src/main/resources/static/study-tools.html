Quick tutorial

How to use the Study Planner

  1. Enter your subject.Type what you want to learn, such as “Biology NCEA Level 3”.
  2. Choose your study period.Enter how many days you have until your test or goal.
  3. Set weekly hours.Tell the planner how many hours you can realistically study each week.
  4. Select your available days.Tick the days you can study. Leave unavailable days unchecked.
  5. Generate your plan.Press “Generate Study Plan” to see your sessions, topics, dates, and time.

Tip: You can change the fields and generate a new plan whenever your schedule changes.
